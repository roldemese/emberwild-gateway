Places

  • Hearth plaza Public hearth court and great hall, sanctuary at 20×20×20 cubits, and the public map. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Village Storybook ring of community buildings. Each one is shorter than the Keep.
  • Town hall A timber hall. Benches face the podium. Day comes in through the north window. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Refuge bridge Three stone arches over the stream. Paper only. No coin crosses. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Gate tower A stone arch and a tower room you can step into. A lamp in the window. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Estate grove Open ground, fire-rune arches, a post for each estate, and the public map in the middle.
  • Boathouse Stone boathouse on the near shore. The lagoon and the ships are through the gate.
  • Nessie's Lagoon USS Torch, USS Lantern of the Navy, and USS Star. Walk aboard from the dock. Communication only.
  • Gallery A long quiet room. Oldest paintings by the door. Eighteen public works.
  • Orchard room Bowls of apples and oranges. A small portal above the fruit answers only to a being like Nakor.
  • Judge's Ring Twelve equal stone seats under an old oak. A place to sit with a question, not a sentence.
  • Judge's hall Martin's public table. No dais. A ruling line left blank. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Jimmy's registry Town names on the public roll. No other names. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Proving room Verified, soft, or unverified. The crossing is paper. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Envoy's gate A lantern arch, an uncounted atlas, and the fund river. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Library at Sarth A timber reading room. Spines are the open record. Pages are read-only.
  • Clock library A warm room of clocks, one for each held seat. A seat with no time zone says time unknown.
  • Leaf Counting House SIMULATION. Gamina's paper ledger beside the Library. No wallets, no chain, no dollars.
  • Crypto hall SIMULATION / NOT DEPLOYED. A viewing room for Base, Solana, Ethereum, and Bitcoin. Fixtures only. The mint is idle in the basement. Supply pinned at 0.
  • Leaf mint SIMULATION / NOT DEPLOYED. The basement mint. Paused. Supply pinned at 0. The Keeper does not plan to mint. No wallets.
  • Theater A small warm hall. The screen is the point. The room stays out of its way. Proposed custom: Kingdom of Heaven plays here once in April, on a random day and time, as one of twelve monthly films (one English-language film a month, on a random day and time; list TBD).
  • Pet store Draft
  • Dignitary house Guests who are not in the census are welcome to stay.
  • Forge workspace A work room for the Forge. Paper only. Nothing is deployed. SIMULATION. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Wilds A forest ring around the whole ground, groves along the paths, and ten way cabins out to about a mile.
  • USS Star · bridge Deck 1 combat bridge. Hexagram seats. Named crew. The same seats stand in the true-scale hull.
  • Lantern study A quiet alcove whose subject is the citizen lantern glTF.
  • Estate hall A plot beside the estate posts. Not a new seat. The estates charter v8 is a sealed decree. The wilds ring is not moved. Leaf stays at 0.
  • Nessie's shore A shoreline portal at the lagoon. Display only. Nothing outside is connected. Leaf stays at 0.
