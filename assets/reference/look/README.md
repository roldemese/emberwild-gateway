# Look references

These photographs are the look for the hearth trip of November 15, 2026. They are not hung in the gallery, and they are not a census. No face in them is a resident. Nothing here is a key, a wallet, or a deployment. The Leaf stays at 0.

Picture-level texture is for the civic core: about an eighth of a mile out from the hearth, including the tower and the Keep. Farther out, the world falls away to the Emberwild texture map.
