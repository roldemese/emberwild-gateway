# USS Torch, and the walkable hulls

Torch is the lagoon's yesterday ship: a Constitution-style frigate you can board from the dock. USS Lantern, the Navy ship, and USS Star, the Starfleet ship, are in the same place, at the sizes already in `FLEET.md`. This note says which numbers are public and which ones were chosen so a person can walk. USS Star is the name given to the Starfleet hull on 11 Nov 2026.

Nothing here is deployed. The ships are still for communication only. The Leaf stays at 0.

## Sources

**USS Constitution, the pattern for Torch.** Naval History and Heritage Command fact sheet, and the Navy fact file, which agree:

- Length overall 305 ft (93 m), bowsprit to spanker.
- 207 ft (63.1 m) from billet head to taffrail, the length used here as length on deck.
- 175 ft (53.3 m) at the waterline.
- Beam 43.5 ft (13.3 m).
- Draft aft, fully loaded in 1812, 24 ft (7.3 m). Draft today 22.5 ft (6.9 m).
- Mainmast, spar deck to truck, 172 ft (54.4 m) as the ship is now.
- 1812 spar deck: twenty-four 32-pound carronades and one 18-pound bow chaser.
- 1812 gun deck: thirty 24-pound long guns.
- 1812 crew about 450. Boats included one 36 ft launch, used to go between ship and shore.

The signal book on the quarterdeck is Thomas Truxtun, *Instructions, Signals and Explanations, Offered to the Consideration of the United States Navy* (1797). The station uses numeral pennants, a flag locker, and halyards to the mizzen peak. That is the fleet's communications fit for this ship, not a second weapons system.

**USS Lantern, Navy.** The sandbox hull is 262 m by 33.5 m, which sits inside the public BBG(X) range. The Congressional Research Service (IF13142, and the USNI summary of it) says the Navy's stated length is 840 to 880 ft and displacement is more than 35,000 tons. 262 m is about 860 ft. USNI News (22 December 2025) reported a draft of 24 to 30 ft, 128 Mk 41 cells, 12 Conventional Prompt Strike missiles, 5-inch guns, and margin for a railgun and directed energy. The flight deck and hangar are described as able to take a V-22. The class is not finished, and no public deck-by-deck plan gives room sizes. The walkable rooms follow the list already in `FLEET.md`.

**USS Star, Starfleet.** Length 170.68 m, beam 134.11 m, height 30.1 m, as the fleet record takes them from Memory Alpha's Defiant-class page and the DS9 technical material. The beam is the overall width across the wings. The walkable pressure hull is narrower. That split is an assumption, called out below. The bridge seats are the ones already in the bridge room. They were not moved. The hull was named USS Star on 11 Nov 2026. The Navy ship is USS Lantern.

## What you can walk

All three clocks, including the ones on the dock, show the same current Pacific time (`America/Los_Angeles`), computed on this machine.

**Torch.** Shore pier, accommodation ladder, gun-deck entry, gun deck, captain's cabin, galley, berth deck, wardroom, lantern locker, sick bay, orlop, hold, magazine, spar deck, quarterdeck and signal station, forecastle. The brass chronometer is in a gimballed box in the cabin.

**Navy Lantern.** Causeway, brow, flight deck, hangar, passageway, nuclear-engineering annex and its chronometer, VLS gallery, CIC, bridge, bridge wing. The cells and gun tubs are inert shapes. They do not fire.

**USS Star.** Causeway, brow, transporter room, engineering and the warp chronometer, the explorer corridor, deflector gallery, bridge with the same seven seats. A gate on that bridge still opens the older bridge room.

## Design assumptions

These are not in the public fact sheets. They are marked so they are not mistaken for measurements.

- Deck pitch is 2.05 m, and the clear height under a deck is about 1.87 m. A frigate's real deck heights were tighter than a 1.70 m eye plus the walking body's headroom. The pitch is opened so that body does not strike the beams. DANFS depth of hold, 14 ft 3 in (4.34 m), is the historical figure and is not the sum of these walkable decks.
- The walkable hold sits just above the world's ground plane. The copper bottom and the 7.3 m draft continue below the waterline as the outside of the hull. You do not walk the submerged part. The world's physics treats open ground as height zero, so a deck far below the waterline would not hold a body.
- Spar deck freeboard, about 8.5 m to the waist, is the result of stacking those walkable decks. It is taller than a frigate's real freeboard.
- Forecastle and quarterdeck are one step above the waist. The break is real on Constitution; the height of the step here is not a published figure.
- Mast stations along the deck, the run of the companion ladders, and which frames bound the cabin, wardroom, sick bay, and magazine are laid out so the rooms connect. The museum publishes the deck names and what each was for. It does not publish a meter-by-meter plan in the fact sheet.
- Sails are nine square sails, courses through topgallants, as a stand-in for the documented 48 sails and about 44,000 square feet. Masts and sails are omitted on the Low quality setting. The hull and the decks stay.
- Carronades are twelve a side, the documented twenty-four, drawn as fittings. Long guns are a row a side rather than all thirty carriages, so the deck stays light. The count on the sign is the documented thirty.
- The Navy hangar clear height, the exact spot of CIC inside the deckhouse, and the run of the passageway are not in the public BBG(X) papers. They follow the room list in `FLEET.md`.
- The Starfleet walkable hull is 36 m in beam. The 134.11 m figure is kept as the wing span, drawn, and not walked. Deck heights inside the 30.1 m ship, and the Jefferies ladder in place of a turbolift, are assumptions. The bridge seat names and stations are not.

## Names

The Starfleet hull is painted USS Star. The Navy hull is painted USS Lantern.
