The guiding principle for the 3D build is in `docs/GUIDING_PRINCIPLE.md`.

Scale used by the runtime: **1 cubit = 0.457 m**. The Keep sanctuary landmark is the open 20×20×20 cubit frame from 1 Kings 6:20. The house is 60×20×30 (1 Kings 6:2) and the porch is 20×10 (1 Kings 6:3).

Avatar height follows the written curve in spirit: about 3.8 cubits (1.74 m).

Pet speech, the two-pets-out cap, the pet store, and the count of 150 estates come from the lunch scrolls. Those scrolls are drafts. The numbers live in `content/rules.ember.json` so they can change without pretending they have been read at the bell.
