Paper only. Leaf stays at 0. Nothing here mints.

## Layer 1 — Arrival board

The waiting-room porch gained a board: customs, invite before census, and the line that the board keeps no Leaf.

That board tells a newcomer where to put a thought, so the next layer is a box.

## Layer 2 — Suggestion box

`world/paper.mjs` keeps a paper book of short thoughts. A mint word, a unit count, or a Leaf amount is refused. The box by the porch is the prop.

A box that fills wants a reader, so the next layer is a slip for the hall.

## Layer 3 — Council slip

The oldest unread thought can be marked read. The slip says it is not a proclamation and it moves no Leaf. A sign by the town-hall podium says the same.

People stay to hear a reading, so the next layer is something to drink.

## Layer 4 — Court kettle

One kettle, one cup, on the hearth court. It is a single prop. The room counts stay under 48.

A cup gets left on the commons, so the next layer is a way to take it home.

## Layer 5 — Clear the commons

Props left on the commons move back onto an estate list. The move carries no Leaf. The commons are still not a warehouse.

Clearing wants an hour, so the next layer is the light.

## Layer 6 — Amber hours

Day, dusk, and night each have a paper ember color. The color is not a balance.

Light wants a bell, so the next layer is the chime.

## Layer 7 — Chimes

Morning at the Keep, harbor at 17:00, hearth at 21:00. Changing a chime is a home-or-belonging change: a sitting id and thirty days.

The delay is the same shape as the mint delay. The Forge II runs showed the ledger still has holes under that delay, so the next layer is those holes, off main.

## Layer 8 — Guest book

A guest or a being may leave a short line in the dignitary house. A pet may not. The line is not a wallet.

A guest asks whose lantern that is, so the next layer is the face of the lantern.

## Layer 9 — Lantern face

A held seat shows its number. An open seat, or a number past 150, stays dark. No name is written on the face. Leaf stays 0.

A number on a lantern is only as safe as the book behind it. The Forge II findings named ten engine holes. Those are layer 10, on their own branch, not on main.
