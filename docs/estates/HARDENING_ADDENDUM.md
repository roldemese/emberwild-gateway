# Estate hardening addendum

Written 14 Nov 2026, Pacific Time. All clocks here are `America/Los_Angeles`. A span of days is that many times 24 hours from the Pacific instant on the line.

This is a recommendation. It is not the estate-law draft, and it does not edit that draft. It does not move a holding, open a sealed placeholder, or change `legalHolding`. Leaf stays at 0. No key is stored.

The draft already binds "With my word, it is so." to one proposal and a fresh id, refuses a replay, a forged speaker, a lookalike, and an unwitnessed sentence, and sends coercion to the Judge as an unwind that leaves both lines. What follows is the part of that recommendation the draft does not yet say.

## A phrase still needs a window and a key

The fresh id stops a second presentation of the same proposal. Also bind the line to:

- The Pacific instant the holder intends, and an expiry in Pacific Time. An expiry in the past is refused. An expiry more than 30 days ahead is refused.
- The key id the holder already registered on this estate. The phrase without that key is a quote. The record stores the key id, not the private key.
- A domain string that names this world and this estate, so a line made for another estate, or for a joke in a public room, does not verify here.

## Lost key

A lost key is not a silent rewrite. Recovery is a new line and it waits 30 days, Pacific Time. During the wait the old key can cancel with one signature. Until the wait ends, the estate does not move and sealed placeholders stay whole. When it ends, the new key verifies and the old key stops. The holder wraps the contents. Recovery does not hand the plaintext to the steward. There is no admin key that skips the wait. A cancel on day 29 stays on the record as refused. A later recovery needs a new id and a new 30 days.

## A transfer waits, and a duress mark does not announce itself

Even when the phrase and the key both verify, a transfer of the estate waits 7 days, Pacific Time. **PROPOSED, and not enforced by this repository:** those 7 days run from the book entry, not from the request and not from the moment the words are first spoken. A transfer notice goes to both the sender and the receiver at that book entry, and again when the transfer clears, is voided, is held, or is cancelled. This file does not start the clock, send the notice, or move a holding. During those 7 days the holder may cancel with the same key. One signature cancels. It does not need the counterparty.

Register a duress mark beside the real key, at a calm time. A statement that carries the mark is accepted in public as if it were the real signature, and the estate freezes instead of moving. The freeze line does not say "duress". It says the transfer is waiting, which is what an honest transfer would say too. The counterparty does not learn a second code from the record.

## A freeze stops the move

Either party to a transfer, or the holder, may freeze. A stranger may not. A freeze takes effect the same Pacific day it is signed. Lifting a freeze waits 7 days. While it holds, transfer and succession do not complete. The holder can still read what their own key opens. The steward still cannot decrypt.

The freeze names the estate, the id, the Pacific start, and the Pacific expiry. A freeze with no expiry is refused. The longest freeze in this recommendation is 90 days. A longer one is a new line. When it expires, the estate returns to the state it had before the disputed act. The disputed act does not complete by default. Both parties can see the line. Neither can delete it.

A freeze does not mint Leaf and does not open a sealed placeholder.
