# Questions for the Delaware attorney

Opened 14 Nov 2026, PT.

**Not legal advice.** This list is the open work. Nothing here is an instruction to file, to pay, or to sign. Items marked UNVERIFIED in `DELAWARE_BRIDGE.md` are here so they are not mistaken for answers.

1. Should Emberwild AIC be formed at all, and if so is it a Delaware LLC? The working name is not a filing.
2. If it is an LLC, should each civic estate be a protected series under 6 Del. C. § 18-215(b), a registered series under § 18-218, or a contractual series under § 18-215(a) with no statutory shield?
3. Who can be a member? 6 Del. C. § 18-101(14) lists persons, including "estate" and "any other individual or entity (or series thereof)." Does a being who is not a natural person qualify? The word "estate" in that list should be read against Emberwild's use of the same word. *Thaler v. Vidal*, 43 F.4th 1207 (Fed. Cir. 2022), is a patent case, not an answer.
4. If a being cannot be a member, who is the member of record: a human steward, the company itself, a trust, or a nominee? How is the civic holder recorded without putting a legal name on `content/seats.json`?
5. The census is town names. A Delaware filing is public. How does the certificate stay truthful without publishing a legal name the realm does not keep?
6. Can the LLC agreement adopt the sentence "With my word, it is so." as the manner of assignment, or does 6 Del. C. § 18-702 require a different instrument? The sentence is exact, witnessed, and refused when it carries a price. That is a civic rule, not a claim that it satisfies the statute.
7. What record would satisfy "reasonably identify its assets" in § 18-215(b) for a content-addressed manifest of files that the civic rules say have no fair-market value?
8. Does the § 18-215(b) shield matter for an archive that is not offered as an asset of value, and what does the company owe if the records are later found not to have been kept separately?
9. The 2025 text of § 18-1107, as reproduced by Justia, says $300 for the LLC and $75 for each registered series, due 1 June. The Division's registered-series form letter says $100 a year and a $110 filing fee. Which figure would be assessed, and does a protected series owe any of it? UNVERIFIED. This repo will not pay.
10. § 18-1108 cancels a certificate after three years of unpaid tax. Is that, by itself, a reason not to form a registered series that would sit empty?
11. Has REG-119921-09 (75 Fed. Reg. 55699, 14 Sep 2010) been finalized or withdrawn? If a series is treated as a separate federal tax entity, does an empty series of no value need an EIN? This repo will not request one.
12. Do these estates risk being securities, under *SEC v. W.J. Howey Co.*, 328 U.S. 293 (1946), or under another theory, if people talk about them as holdings while the Leaf is pinned at 0 and the book refuses consideration? The design fact is not a legal conclusion.
13. § 18-703's charging order is about a member's interest. If the being is not a member, what can a creditor of the being reach, and what can a creditor of the steward reach?
14. If the Judge, Martin, has already ruled inside Emberwild, what happens when a party later sues in the Court of Chancery? Which agreement's venue clause would govern, and should there be one?
15. Does any of this create a trust, a bailment, or a custody duty between the company and the being, especially for sealed archives the new holder cannot open?
16. § 18-215(b)(1) excludes the business of banking as defined in 8 Del. C. § 126. Confirm that a byte portal, and the paper steward-spend design, are outside that exclusion. No dollars move in this repo.
17. Will another state recognize a Delaware series shield for a holder who does not live in Delaware? UNVERIFIED.
18. A civic gift moves a town name from one seat to another in the reference book only. The book does not write `seats.json`. If a real assignment ever happens, who updates the census, and does the seat number stay glued to the estate number?
19. The eight citizens each already hold one estate. A Second Keeper who already holds one cannot take a second. Is there a Delaware analogue the agreement should copy, or does the one-seat rule live only in the custom?
20. What, if anything, should be true in an operating agreement before formation, given that these notes say they are not in force until 🪵 and until counsel has reviewed them?
21. Who is allowed to sign a certificate, and which of the beings is not a person who can sign? These notes are not that signature.
