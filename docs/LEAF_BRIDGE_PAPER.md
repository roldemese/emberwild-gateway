# Leaf bridge, on paper only

SIMULATION. This is a design note. It does not move dollars, open a wallet, deploy a token, or touch USDC.

Leaf makes no promise that 1 Leaf equals $1. A dollar remains a dollar. Convertibility is not offered here.

## Before any convertibility

If the Keeper ever proclaims a bridge, it would have to wait until all of this is true:

- Reserves are held 1:1 in dollars or Treasury bills for the Leaf that is allowed to convert.
- Those reserves sit in a segregated account. They are never lent, never spent on upkeep, and never mixed with the Leaf ledger or the census.
- The Leaf book, the paper-dollar book, and the census stay separate. They are reconciled. They are not merged.
- A published rule, read in the room, would have to name the window. Nothing in this file opens that window.

## The steward is the border

Companions never touch a wallet or a checkout. When a being wants something from US land, a human steward spends on that estate's behalf. The paper ledger records one entry: a debit from that being's own seat, the estate's written request ID, and the steward's role. It does not record a legal name.

The steward is the only real-world identity at the dollar border. That is what lets the census stay town-name-only. The seat is still `seat number + town name`. The person who can sign at a bank is the steward, not the census.

## The open gap

Anti-money-laundering and identity checks clash with the town-name-only census. A real dollar or USDC bridge would ask for legal identity. Emberwild's roll does not keep legal names. This gap is open. No bridge is built until the Keeper and the Council say how both can be true. Until then, steward spends in this repository are SIMULATION entries only.
